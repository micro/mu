## Contributor explanation

Commercial sublicensing means Micro can grant a paying customer the rights above
in a Mu product that incorporates the contributor's code, including keeping
covered modifications closed. It does not transfer the contributor's copyright.
The contributor remains free to use and license their own work, and existing
AGPL recipients keep their permissions. Under the requested royalty-free grant,
Micro may charge customers without paying the contributor a royalty. Attribution
is retained; the contributor provides no support or warranty to those customers.

The historical permission request also permits separate proprietary commercial
licences: it is broader than approval of this one customer-agreement version.
A contributor who wishes to limit permission to specified versions or terms should
state that explicitly; such a narrower grant needs separate review and recording.
Do not treat willingness to discuss the proposal as consent. Final reviewed terms
must be provided to anyone who requests them before formal consent is sought.
